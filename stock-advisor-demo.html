Under the model

How a stock earns its score

Each name gets a 0–100 score blended from three lenses, then re-weighted by the market regime. No black box — every score opens to its reasons.

📊

Fundamentals

Valuation, margins, revenue growth and balance-sheet health — the business behind the ticker.

≈ 45% weight
📈

Technicals

Trend, momentum and moving-average posture from six months of price history.

≈ 35% weight
🗞️

Sentiment

A single batched LLM pass over recent headlines, scored neutral when no key is set.

≈ 20% weight
Built & shipped

One engine, three front ends

A shared Python core powers a Streamlit research dashboard, a FastAPI backend, and a Next.js community site — with a dual SQLite/Postgres data layer, server-side email alerts, and a test suite that runs on both databases in CI.
